Two days of making, sharing and finding your next spark.

Explore hands-on workshops, conversations with creative founders and a marketplace of independent makers. Come curious and leave with new ideas and new connections.

  • Studio

    Hands-on workshops

    90-minute sessions in poster printing, collage, zines and a collaborative mural. Up to 30 people each, first come, first served, and included with every pass.

  • Main Stage

    Talks with creative founders

    Conversations on building a business, finding your visual voice and turning a side project into a studio. Talks run 45 minutes and seat up to 150.

  • Maker Market

    Independent makers

    Vendor booths are open 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM and food vendors until 4:00 PM, both days. Food is sold separately. Workshop full? This is the place to wander, browse and meet people.
